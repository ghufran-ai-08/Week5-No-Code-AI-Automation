# AI Customer Support Automation

## Workflow

```text
Google Form
     ↓
Google Sheets
     ↓
Make Trigger
     ↓
Google Gemini
     ↓
Gmail


# Trigger

A new row appearing in the Google Sheets response spreadsheet.

The row is created automatically when a customer submits the
Google Form.

# AI Processing

Gemini receives the customer's:

Name
Email
Issue type
Issue description

The AI then:

Categorizes the issue
Determines urgency
Summarizes the problem
Drafts a professional support response
Final Action

Gmail sends an email containing the original customer information
and the AI-generated support analysis.

# Purpose

The automation demonstrates how Generative AI can be integrated into
an everyday business workflow without building a complete custom
application.
